Exam Tips

* AWS Managed Microsoft AD is best for full AD support in AWS, including trust
relationships.

+ AD Connector is ideal if you want to reuse on-premises AD without storing AD data in
AWS.

« Simple AD is for small, cost-sensitive environments with basic AD needs.
